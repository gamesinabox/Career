Goal — Ecommerce Manager

Where this is heading
Sydney median sits around $100,000–$128,000, roughly $79,000–$145,000 depending on scope
  • 3–5 years' ecommerce experience is the typical bar in current listings
  • Demonstrated team or people leadership — even informal leadership counts if you can describe it concretely
  • Ownership of trade performance/revenue targets, with reporting delivered to senior stakeholders
  • Shopify Plus exposure (Flow, Scripts, B2B) — most Manager listings assume Plus, not standard Shopify
  • Comfort with budget or commercial decisions, not just channel-level KPIs
  • AI/automation fluency is now a genuine salary premium in 2026 listings, and retail media (Amazon Ads and similar) is one of the fastest-growing skill asks even outside pure marketplace roles
  • The core stack from Stage 2 (GA4, SEO, UX, online sales, digital marketing) is assumed, not a differentiator anymore

How to build leadership evidence before you have the title

  1. Take one project end-to-end and own the decisions — the parts finder rollout is a strong example. Document what you decided, why, and what changed as a result.
  2. Manage a relationship, not just a task — a freelancer, an agency, or a supplier. Being the person who directs someone else's work is leadership, regardless of title.
  3. Ask to be the one presenting results — even a five-minute update to your manager on how a campaign or launch performed builds the "reports to senior stakeholders" evidence Manager roles ask for.
